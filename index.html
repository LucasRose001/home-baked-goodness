Things people ask

Answers first, quotes second.

How much does a typical deck cost in Saskatoon?+
For a 200 sq ft build with basic railings, most homeowners land between $6,000 (pressure-treated) and $14,500 (composite, fully loaded). Use the estimator above to model your specific build — those numbers are within 8% of our real quotes.
Do I need a permit for a deck in Saskatoon?+
Any deck over 2 ft off the ground or attached to your house requires a permit. We handle the filing, drawings, and inspection scheduling as part of every build — you never touch City Hall.
Can you build in winter?+
We book design and permits Nov–Feb, and start ground-breaking as soon as frost is out — usually early April. Deposits secure your slot in the build calendar; most spring slots are gone by January.
What does the 10-year warranty actually cover?+
Everything we did with our hands: framing, fasteners, board attachment, railings, stairs, level, seams. It doesn't cover material defects — those are covered by the manufacturer's warranty, which is usually longer (25 yr on composite). We handle claims on both.
Do you tear out my old deck too?+
Yes. Teardown, haul-away, footing removal — $600–$1,200 depending on size and how the old one was attached. Included as a line item on your quote so you're not surprised.
Can I see one in person before I commit?+
Almost always yes. We keep a rotating list of recent clients happy to show their deck to a serious prospect. Ask during your consultation and we'll match you to a build near you.
